# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kautilya AI to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, caste, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what's best for the community and the project
- owning our mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about a person or group
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or personal and political attacks
- publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and chat) and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **hello@revealiq.in**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from project spaces. They may remove or edit comments, commits, code and issues that don't align with this Code of Conduct.
